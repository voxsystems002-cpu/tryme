    The card renders identically on any machine, including ones that ship no cuneiform font.

    The eight-glyph line is a 500-byte subset of Noto Sans Cuneiform, embedded in the page as a data URI. tools/check_card.py fails the build if any printed glyph is missing from it.

  • R2tools/check_contrast.py

    Contrast is measured, not eyeballed.

    Five text/surface pairs are computed with the WCAG relative-luminance formula. Lowest is 6.15:1 against a 4.5:1 floor.

  • R3index.html

    The head data and the back of the card cannot drift apart.

    The back face is not hand-written; it is printed from the same JSON the head carries, and the checker compares both copies byte-for-byte.
