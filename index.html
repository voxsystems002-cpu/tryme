The language — invented words, real letters

Iaconian is three words. It has no speaker community, no history and no authority; it was made today. What it does have is a script that existed long before it: Ugaritic, a consonantal alphabet cut into tablets at Ras Shamra on the Syrian coast around the 15th–12th century BCE, deciphered in 1930–31, and encoded in Unicode in 2006 at U+10380–U+1039F. So the letters can be checked by anyone with a font. The words cannot be checked by anyone, which is the honest difference.

  • 𐎚 to the hold. that which is kept fixed and does not move. U+1039A
  • 𐎖𐎆 qo a path that has been walked before. a line already crossed. U+10396 …
  • 𐎐𐎎𐎚𐎜𐎐 nemtun to carry a thing back to the place it was found. U+10390 …

Line reads to · qo · nemtun — The hold, the walked path, carry it back.

Why those letters

  • U+1039A UGARITIC LETTER TO — the letter that is also the word for a hold
  • U+10396 UGARITIC LETTER QOPA — opens the word for a path already walked
  • U+10390 UGARITIC LETTER NUN — the letter the returning word opens and closes on
  • not a coincidental repeat — NUN opens and closes nemtun because the word means returning; TO is a single letter that is also a whole word, because the word for a hold is a thing that stays put.

What the language is not

  • not a decipherment, and not a translation of anyone's inscription
  • not Ugaritic: these are not Ugaritic words, in any attestation
  • no native speakers, no community, no authority. it is three words made today
  • the romanisation is ours and the vowels are an editorial choice
  • no claim that the sentence has been found anywhere outside this repository

Where to check the real half

  • the script and its Unicode encoding: The Unicode Standard, block U+10380–U+1039F
  • the archaeology and decipherment: Ras Shamra / Ugarit publications, 1929–1931
  • a font that covers the block: Noto Sans Ugaritic, SIL Open Font License 1.1
